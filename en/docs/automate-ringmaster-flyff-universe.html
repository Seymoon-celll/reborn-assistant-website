Why automate your Ringmaster

The Ringmaster (often abbreviated RM) is the iconic support class of Flyff Universe: the healing and buffing branch of the Assist, available at level 60, whose third class, the Seraph, arrived with the Muran's Awakening expansion (v1.5.0, November 2025). Its role is clear: strengthen allies with buffs (Protect, Spirit Fortune, Geburah Tiphreth, and Assist buffs such as Heap Up, Beef Up or Mental Sign) and heal the party with Heal Rain. On paper, it's simple. In practice, it's one of the most repetitive classes to play during long farm sessions: you spend a good part of your time recasting your buffs as soon as they expire, recasting Geburah Tiphreth, which only lasts 30 seconds plus 0.15 seconds per point of INT, chaining Heal Rains and watching your MP on top of the party's HP.

Automation does not replace your judgment. It offloads mechanical routines from your keyboard: the full rebuff cycle, Geburah Tiphreth, the periodic Heal Rain, MP potions. Reborn Assistant sees neither the screen nor the health bars: it presses the keys you configured, at the pace you chose. Your hands stay free for the moments that actually matter: a party member going down, a pack of monsters getting out of hand, a boss targeting the RM first.

💡 Typical use case You support a partner through a 4-hour farm, for example in Darkon 3, home of the Glaphans (levels 102 to 109). With 300 INT, your buffs at max skill level last 250 + 600 = 850 seconds, about 14 minutes. Without automation: around 17 full rebuff cycles, plus nearly 200 Geburah Tiphreth recasts (75 seconds each). With Reborn Assistant configured once, those cycles run in the background. You stay at your screen and step in if the composition changes or someone dies.

Prerequisites

Before activating anything, lay solid foundations. A useful reminder: in Flyff Universe, F1 to F9 show one of your 9 shortcut bars, and keys 1 to 0 fire the slots of the bar currently shown.

  • Reborn Assistant Premium installed (the free version only covers the basic heal macro). See the installation guide.
  • Your rotation buffs on your 2nd bar (the one F2 shows), slots 1 to 9 — that's the bar Reborn Assistant's rotation goes through.
  • Geburah Tiphreth and your potions on your 1st bar (F1), the one the rotation brings you back to at the end of each cycle.
  • Heal Rain on slot 0 of both bars: the rotation never presses 0, so Heal Rain fires whether your 1st or 2nd bar is shown when the heal triggers. Keys can be rebound in Options › Controls; if you'd rather use a letter, avoid T, which opens the Status window.
  • A supply of MP potions (Refreshers): every Ringmaster spell costs MP, not FP — automation does not invent resources.
  • A formed party and a spot where it stays together: Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth only affect party members within 13 meters of you.

Choose the buffs to automate

Not all buffs are automated the same way. What matters to Reborn Assistant is how long they last: the extension cannot see when a buff expires, it only follows the interval you give it. Most Assist and Ringmaster buffs last 2 min 30 s to 4 min 10 s at base depending on skill level, plus 2 seconds per point of the caster's INT, with no cap (Geburah Tiphreth is the exception). Almost all of them are single-target: they apply to the selected character. Flyff Universe guides for full-support Ringmasters recommend maxing Protect, Spirit Fortune and Geburah Tiphreth first.

Buffs to keep up in the rotation (single-target)

  • Protect (Ringmaster) — Defense and Magic Defense +10% at level 10
  • Spirit Fortune (Ringmaster) — +150 damage at level 10
  • Heap Up, Beef Up, Mental Sign, Cannon Ball (Assist) — STA, STR, INT and DEX +20 respectively at level 20
  • Patience (Assist) — max HP +10% at level 20
  • Haste (Assist) — attack speed +15% and casting time −10% at level 20
  • Cat's Reflex (Assist) — block rate +10% at level 20
  • Accuracy (Assist) — hit rate +20% at level 20
  • Quick Step (Assist) — movement speed +20% at level 20

Each of these buffs also gets stronger with your INT (for example +0.04 STA per point of INT for Heap Up), up to a cap reached at 500 INT. Their duration keeps growing. At max skill level, it equals 250 seconds plus 2 seconds per point of INT:

The odd one out: Geburah Tiphreth

Geburah Tiphreth is the Ringmaster's only party buff: at level 10, it gives Attack +10%, attack speed +15% and casting time −10% to you and to party members within 13 meters. But it only lasts 30 seconds plus 0.15 seconds per point of INT, whatever its level, and has no cooldown. It is far too short for the rotation: give it its own macro (see below).

Skills to keep on manual

These skills answer a specific situation. Since Reborn Assistant cannot see what happens on screen, keep them under your fingers:

  • Resurrection — revives a dead player; never automated, a timer doesn't know who went down
  • Holycross — debuff on an enemy: the next hit it takes deals double damage (5 seconds)
  • Holyguard — immunity to critical hits for 2 to 5 seconds at base, with a cooldown of 100 seconds at level 1 and 45 at level 10
  • Gvur Tialla — removes all debuffs from the target
  • Barrier of Life — the target blocks every hit for 1.2 to 2 seconds, with an 18-second cooldown

Configure the buff rotation

This is the heart of the system. Reborn Assistant can press a fixed sequence F2 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → F1 at regular intervals. In the game, F2 shows your 2nd shortcut bar, 1 to 9 fire its slots, and F1 brings you back to your 1st bar. Here's how to align it with your RM:

1
Prepare your 2nd bar

Open the Skill window (K key), show your 2nd bar with F2 and drag your buffs onto it in this order:

  • Key 1 → Protect
  • Key 2 → Spirit Fortune
  • Key 3 → Heap Up
  • Key 4 → Patience
  • Key 5 → Haste
  • Keys 6 to 9 → four other buffs chosen for your target (Beef Up, Mental Sign, Cannon Ball, Cat's Reflex, Accuracy…)

These buffs are single-target: each cycle casts them on whoever is selected at that moment. Keep your partner selected while farming.

2
Enable rotation in Reborn Assistant

In the Reborn panel, Premium tab:

  • Toggle on "Sequence Rotation"
  • Set the interval between cycles slightly below the duration of your shortest buff (see the table above): for example 13 minutes at 300 INT, for buffs lasting 14 min 10 s. If you add Prevention, a self-only auto-heal, its base duration stays 2 min 30 s at every level: it then sets the interval.
  • Set the delay between keys to 1 second: Assist and Ringmaster buffs have a 1-second casting time (less with Haste, Geburah Tiphreth or gear that reduces casting time)
3
Test a full cycle

Start with CTRL+A. Watch the activity log: you should see F2 pressed → 1 pressed → 2 pressed… sequentially, with ~1 second between each. If a buff doesn't fire, check in-game: most often you are out of MP or the wrong target is selected. These buffs have no cooldown (Prevention has one, but it is much shorter than your interval).

Program the automatic Heal Rain

Heal Rain runs in parallel with the buff rotation. It's an instant party heal: with a 0.5-second cast, it heals party members within 13 meters of you (200 HP + 8 × INT at level 1, up to 650 HP + 8.18 × INT at level 10) for 80 to 170 MP. The game data gives it no duration and no cooldown: the pace is up to you. Reborn Assistant has a dedicated heal section in the panel, with a fully configurable key and interval.

1
Assign Heal Rain to a key

Drag Heal Rain onto slot 0 of your 1st and 2nd bars (see the prerequisites). Then enter that same key, 0, in the "Key" field of the heal section of the Reborn panel.

2
Set the interval, then enable

The game imposes no interval: choose it based on the damage your party takes and your MP supply. The shorter it is, the more you heal, and the more you spend: at level 10, one Heal Rain every 5 seconds costs about 2,000 MP per minute. Start with a cautious value, watch, then adjust. Switch the toggle ON. The extension will press your key every X seconds, in parallel with the buff rotation and the other systems, whether the party is hurt or not, since it doesn't read health bars.

⚠️ Watch your positioning Heal Rain instantly heals party members within 13 meters of you at the moment it fires: it is not a zone that stays on the ground. An ally too far away at that instant, or a player who isn't in your party, gets nothing. Stay within range of your allies or set a fixed rally point for the group.

Macros, Pet, Follow, Auto-click

Beyond the buff rotation and the heal, Reborn Assistant Premium offers four complementary systems that you enable independently as needed.

3 custom macros

Each macro presses a single key at a configurable interval (seconds or minutes). One macro = one key, not a sequence. To combine several keys in order, use the main buff rotation.

Typical uses for a Ringmaster:

  • Macro 1 — Geburah Tiphreth's key, with an interval just below its duration: for example 60 seconds at 300 INT, where it lasts 75 seconds
  • Macro 2 — an MP potion key (Refresher) every 20 to 30 seconds to keep your MP high; adjust to your consumption, as Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth are your most expensive spells. A Refresher Hold, a premium in-game item, gives unlimited MP for 1 hour and makes this macro unnecessary while it lasts.
  • Macro 3 — an emergency HP potion key every 60 seconds, as a backup to the main heal. It fires even when your HP is full: the extension can't see your gauge.

If a macro comes due during a buff sequence, it runs automatically at the end of the buff cycle, one at a time, with the same delay as your buffs. Since the rotation ends with F1, put your macro keys on your 1st bar.

Automatic pet (Premium)

The extension summons your pet before each buff cycle and puts it away after. Handy so you don't forget your pet during a long farm, without adding a manual key to your rotation.

Automatic follow (Premium)

Repeated press of a dedicated key (default F in the extension) every 5 seconds. In Flyff Universe, the action that follows the selected target is on Z by default, according to the third-party tools that use it: check it in Options › Controls, especially on an AZERTY keyboard, and enter exactly the same key in the extension. Ideal for following your partner while you buff and heal: since they stay selected, they also stay the target of your buffs. Only the key is configurable; the 5-second interval is fixed.

Mouse auto-click (Premium)

Clicks at a fixed screen position every 2 seconds. Turn it on by clicking the 🖱 icon in the panel header, then click where you want the clicks to happen. A blinking red dot shows the active position. Click the ✕ next to the dot to stop.

Optimization and common mistakes

  • Key delay too short — if buffs skip, raise the delay between keys to 1.5 or 2 seconds. Better a slightly slower cycle that always completes.
  • Respect the casting time — every Assist or Ringmaster buff takes 1 second to cast before reductions. A delay of at least 1 second between two keys lets each spell finish.
  • MP running out mid-cycle — the extension keeps pressing, but the game no longer casts anything, and Reborn Assistant can't see it. A cycle of nine buffs at max level already costs nearly 600 MP. Enable the MP potion macro or raise your INT: each point of INT gives the Ringmaster 16.2 max MP.
  • Party members out of range — the periodic heal only heals party members within 13 meters of you, and Geburah Tiphreth has the same range. Talk to your group: they need to stay in range.
  • Wrong target when the cycle fires — almost all your buffs (Protect, Spirit Fortune, Heap Up, Haste…) are single-target and land on whoever is selected when the rotation fires. Check your target before each cycle.

Legal aspects — what you need to know

Automating repetitive actions through an extension may break the Terms of Service of Flyff Universe, developed by Gala Lab. The game's report menu has a reason dedicated to bots, and the client contains a ban message aimed at cheating software that tells players to stop using any external tool. Nothing guarantees that an extension simulating key presses is tolerated: read the official Terms of Service before automating anything. Reborn Assistant does not read game memory, does not send network packets, does not modify the client: it's strictly a simulation of keys you would press manually.
