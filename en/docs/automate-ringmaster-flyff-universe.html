Why automate your Ringmaster

The Ringmaster (often abbreviated RM) is the iconic support class of Flyff Universe. Its role is clear: keep the group alive through buffs (Heart of Power, Beef Up, Mental Sign…) that last between 5 and 30 minutes, and heal damage via Heal Rain in AOE. On paper, it's simple. In practice, it's one of the most exhausting classes to play during long farm sessions: you spend 80% of your time rebuffing every 5 minutes, relaunching Heal Rain every 10-15 seconds, and watching the FP bars of every group member.

Automation does not replace your judgment. It offloads mechanical routines from your keyboard: the full rebuff sequence, the periodic heal, FP potion usage. Your hands stay free for the moments that actually matter: an imminent wipe, a wave going sideways, a boss targeting the RM first.

💡 Typical use case You start a 4-hour farm on Glaphan or Asteria. Without automation: 240 minutes ÷ 5 minutes = 48 manual rebuff cycles. With Reborn Assistant configured: 1 cycle, the rest runs in the background. You only step in if the composition changes or someone dies.

Prerequisites

Before activating anything, lay solid foundations:

  • Reborn Assistant Premium installed (the free version only covers the basic heal macro). See the installation guide.
  • Your Ringmaster buffs assigned to keys F2 → 0 in logical order (Heart of Power first, AOE buffs next, single-target last).
  • Heal Rain on the § key (or any dedicated key) — the key Reborn Assistant will loop on.
  • A stack of FP potions in inventory — automation does not invent resources.
  • A stable farm zone — avoid spots with forced teleports or pushing mobs.

Identify your Ringmaster sequences

Not all sequences are equal. Here's the typical hierarchy of a full-support RM build:

Essential sequences (rotation every 5 min)

  • Heart of Power (HoP) — STA + attack boost, mandatory
  • Beef Up — flat STA, increases group max HP
  • Mental Sign — INT + magic boost for Mages / AOE Knights
  • Speed Pep — attack speed, huge for Blades / Rangers
  • Cat's Reflex — DODGE, useful in tough zones

Situational sequences (manual activation)

  • Resurrection — never auto, case by case
  • Holy Cross — elemental damage bonus, context-dependent

Configure the buff rotation

This is the heart of the system. Reborn Assistant can press a fixed sequence F2 → 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 → 0 → F1 at regular intervals. Here's how to align it with your RM:

1
Prepare your sequence slots

Drag your sequences from the skill bar to the Flyff hotkey bar in this order:

  • Key 1 → Heart of Power
  • Key 2 → Beef Up
  • Key 3 → Mental Sign
  • Key 4 → Speed Pep
  • Key 5 → Cat's Reflex
  • Keys 6 to 0 → other available buffs or empty
2
Enable rotation in Reborn Assistant

In the Reborn panel, Premium tab:

  • Toggle on "Sequence Rotation"
  • Set the interval between cycles to the duration of your shortest buff (typically 5 min)
  • Set the delay between keys to 1 second (lets sequence animation finish)
3
Test a full cycle

Start with CTRL+A. Watch the activity log: you should see F2 pressed → 1 pressed → 2 pressed… sequentially, with ~1 second between each. If a sequence doesn't fire, it's probably a cooldown or missing FP — check in-game visually.

Program the periodic Heal Rain

Heal Rain should run in parallel with the buff rotation. Reborn Assistant offers a fixed 15-second periodic macro, perfectly calibrated for Heal Rain's AOE duration (~12-15 seconds).

1
Assign Heal Rain to the § key

By default, Reborn Assistant's periodic macro triggers the § key. Drag Heal Rain onto this key in Flyff.

2
Enable the periodic macro

Toggle "Periodic Macro" in the Premium panel. The system will call Heal Rain every 15 seconds, with automatic storage.

⚠️ Watch your positioning Heal Rain is a self-centered AOE. If you constantly move (kite or reposition), your Heal Rain won't cover your DPS. Stay static or set a fixed rally point for the group.

Custom macros (DPS, support, emergency)

Beyond rotation and heal, Reborn Assistant Premium offers 3 independent macros. Here's how to leverage them for a Ringmaster:

Macro 1 — Pet feeding

If you have a pet that needs regular feeding, program a sequence like k,h every 30 seconds. Prevents your pet from dying during long farms.

Macro 2 — FP potion stack

Configure a sequence like y every 20 seconds to keep FP above 80%. Adjust based on consumption and Heal Rain cadence.

Macro 3 — Anti-AFK

A sequence like up,down or space every 4-5 minutes prevents AFK disconnects during long calm periods. Use with discernment.

Optimization and common mistakes

  • Key delay too short — if sequences skip, increase delay to 1.5-2 seconds. Better a slightly slower cycle that always completes.
  • Don't exceed the global cooldown (GCD) — Flyff has a GCD. Too fast = sequences absorbed into the void. The default 1s respects this GCD.
  • FP running out mid-cycle — your rotation stops silently. Enable the FP potion macro or boost your INT/MP.
  • Group members outside AOE — periodic heal only covers players within ~12-15m. Communicate with the group.
  • Target lost after mob death — not a problem for the RM, but watch your single-target sequences.
