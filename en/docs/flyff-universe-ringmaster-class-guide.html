What is a Ringmaster?

The Ringmaster (RM) is, alongside the Billposter, one of the two classes the Assist can pick at its second job change in Flyff Universe. It is the healing and buffing branch: its main job is keeping a group alive and making it stronger. That doesn't make it a mere healer, though: Madrigal Inside's guides (Basic AoE and Basic HnR Ringmaster Guide) also describe AoE and Hit-and-Run builds that let it level solo. Its nine Ringmaster skills are all cast with a stick and cost MP, never FP. Five of them (Merkaba Hanzelrusha, Spirit Fortune, Geburah Tiphreth, Gvur Tialla and Barrier of Life) also need Bless Posters equipped (sold for 50 penya each): without them, the skill won't cast.

Class path

  • Levels 1 to 15: Vagrant, the class every character starts as.
  • Level 15: Assist (first job change, levels 15 to 60). This is where you learn the heals and buffs the Ringmaster keeps.
  • Level 60: Ringmaster (from level 60 until your third job change), or Billposter if you'd rather fight with your fists.
  • Level 166: Seraph, the third job added by the Muran's Awakening expansion (v1.5.0, November 27, 2025), up to the level cap, now 190. You switch through a quest ("To Awakening", from [Sage] Radion) that opens at level 166 according to Madrigal Inside. Its skills require levels 166 to 175: Kyrie Eleison and Divinecross (166), Gloria Patri (170), Hammer of Judgement (175)…

What a Ringmaster does

  • Heal: Heal (one ally, 650 + INT × 5.88 HP at level 20, 0.15 s cast), Circle Healing (party members within 15 m) and Heal Rain (party members within 13 m, from level 70). All of these heals grow with INT.
  • Buff: the Assist's nine stat buffs (STR, STA, DEX, INT, max HP, attack speed, movement speed, block, hit rate) plus the Ringmaster's own: Protect (defense), Spirit Fortune (damage), Holyguard (crit immunity) and Geburah Tiphreth, the party buff.
  • Revive: Resurrection, an Assist skill, brings a dead player back and reduces their EXP loss.
  • React: Gvur Tialla removes every debuff from an ally, Barrier of Life makes them block every hit for 1.2 to 2 s, and Holycross makes an enemy take double damage from the next hit it receives.

The Ringmaster's role in a group

Three main play contexts:

⚔️

Group leveling and farming

You back up one or more damage dealers. Your job: renew buffs before they expire, recast Geburah Tiphreth, which lasts far less than the others, heal with Heal Rain and Heal, and watch your MP. Stay within 13 m: that's the range of both Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth. The rebuffing part is highly repetitive: see "Rotation and pacing" for what an automated key sequence can and can't do, and for the game's rules on it.

🐉

Dungeons & bosses

Instance dungeons open by difficulty tier, each tied to a level range (per the universe-doc community documentation): Easy 16-30, Medium 31-60, Hard 61-90, Extra Hard 91-120, Nightmare 121-140, Cursed 141-165 and Infernal 166-190. Leren Chasm (Flaris) is available from Easy, Rhisis Catacombs (Garden of Rhisis) from Medium, Envy Depths (Darkon 1 and 2) from Hard, Guardiane Sanctuary (Darkon 3) from Extra Hard, Storm Peak (Azria) from Nightmare and The Wilds (Coral Island) from Cursed. Ankou's Asylum (Kaillun) only exists on Infernal, so from level 166: it's Seraph content. Against bosses such as the Caveman Chief or the Crystal Demon Lord, the rhythm changes: more targeted heals, defensive skills to time well (Holyguard, Barrier of Life, Gvur Tialla) and sometimes a Resurrection to plan for. Keep your hands on the heals.

🏆

Guild Siege and FWC

Besides the main Guild Siege, which guilds apply for (only the 10 highest bids take part), the game's texts include level-bracket Guild Sieges: the level 60 bracket is for characters who haven't made their second job change (so a Ringmaster can't enter it), the level 80 bracket for players up to level 80 (so a level 60-80 Ringmaster can play it), and the level 165 bracket for characters who haven't made their third job change: that's the Ringmaster's bracket until they become a Seraph (a Seraph can't enter). The siege rules remove all buffs when you enter the arena, so plan to rebuff inside. The Flyff World Championship (FWC) has been held every year since 2023; the 2026 edition started on April 23, 2026.

Ringmaster skills

The Ringmaster learns nine skills between levels 60 and 100. Each skill level costs 3 points (2 for Assist skills), all of them require a stick and all of them cost MP; Merkaba Hanzelrusha, Spirit Fortune, Geburah Tiphreth, Gvur Tialla and Barrier of Life also use up equipped Bless Posters. Buffs: what they really raise

The Ringmaster keeps the Assist's buffs and adds its own. Every buff in this section targets a single player, except Geburah Tiphreth, which hits the whole party within 13 m. The Assist's nine stat buffs last 150 s (2:30) at level 1 and 250 s (4:10) at level 20; Protect and Spirit Fortune last 160 to 250 s. In every case, add 2 s per point of the caster's INT, with no cap. Example: at 300 INT, a max-level buff lasts 250 + 300 × 2 = 850 s, a little over 14 minutes. Geburah Tiphreth is the exception: 30 s + 0.15 s per INT point, whatever its level (75 s at 300 INT).

Madrigal Inside's support guide calls three of the Ringmaster's own buffs essential for any support build:

Full Support, AoE or Hit-and-Run — Which build to choose?

Flyff Universe guides describe three builds:

BuildStat spreadWhat it's for
Full Support15 STR / 15 STA / 15 DEX, everything else in INTBuffing and healing a group or a leveling partner, with buffs and heals at their strongest.
AoE (facetank)Mostly INT, with about 50 STA (Madrigal Inside, 80 at most) to 100-120 STA (Flyff Universe Wiki)Leveling solo by gathering monsters around you, with Merkaba, Stonehand and Prevention.
Hit-and-Run (HnR)Full INT (same as Full Support: 15 STR / 15 STA / 15 DEX, the rest in INT)Flexible: the same stats work for support, dungeons and solo leveling, with no reset. Effective up to around level 120 according to Madrigal Inside.

What all three share: INT. Your heals, the strength and duration of your buffs, the damage of Merkaba Hanzelrusha (INT feeds the skill's attack with a factor of 4 at level 1 to 4.18 at level 10, before the other multipliers) and your MP pool all depend on it — each INT point gives a Ringmaster 16.2 max MP. The 500 INT figure comes up a lot: that's where every INT-based buff bonus hits its cap, with the skills at max level (Heap Up: 0.04 × 500 = +20 extra STA; Spirit Fortune: 0.4 × 500 = +200 extra damage; Geburah Tiphreth: attack +20% in total). Beyond that, INT keeps raising your heals, your MP and your buff durations.

Skill order

  • Full Support (per Madrigal Inside): Holycross first (it has a single level, costing 3 skill points; required for Spirit Fortune), then max Protect, Spirit Fortune and Geburah Tiphreth. Geburah Tiphreth also requires Holyguard 4; beyond that, Holyguard matters mostly when you support an AoE player (unless you're heading for Seraph, see below). Heal at level 3 is enough to support a single player once INT is high: at 500 INT it restores about 3,010 HP versus 3,590 at level 20, with a 0.55 s cast instead of 0.15 s.
  • AoE (per Madrigal Inside): max Merkaba Hanzelrusha from level 60; keep Prevention (an automatic heal when your HP drops below 15% in PvE) and Stonehand (a stun chance on every attack, 12% in PvE at level 20; it requires Temping Hole 4, a knuckle attack: budget 8 points) maxed; Protect early; Heal around level 3. Keep Geburah Tiphreth, Heap Up, Patience and Cat's Reflex up, and save Holyguard for big pulls. From level 105, Madrigal Inside points to the Darkon 3 desert with the Rimyth (male) or Remingster (female) set.
  • Hit-and-Run (per Madrigal Inside): Merkaba Hanzelrusha for damage, Moon Beam maxed, Barrier of Life for safety (from level 100) and Geburah Tiphreth (from level 75) as the key buff.

Getting ready for Seraph

Seraph skills cost 10 points per level, and some of them require Ringmaster skills: Kyrie Eleison, the prerequisite of Gloria Patri, Blessing of the Wise, Flow of Salvation and Hammer of Judgement, needs Holyguard 5; Soul of Rhisis, which opens Celestial Barrier, Heaven's Step and Sacrifice, needs Protect 5. Heal matters too: every Heal level above 15 adds 200 HP to Gloria Patri's heal (+1,000 HP with Heal 20). Gloria Patri and Flow of Salvation heals scale with STA, not INT. Finally, Kyrie Eleison, Gloria Patri and the other hymns are chanted: casting another skill during a hymn cancels it. The one exception is another hymn, once you have learned the Hymn - Liberation passive (level 166, 10 points), which lets you chant several hymns at the same time.

Before level 60: the Assist

Madrigal Inside describes three ways to level an Assist: Hit-and-Run with Moon Beam (full INT, stick; available from level 15, it deals damage over time and makes the target take up to 8% more damage at skill level 20), STR AoE with a knuckle and a shield (Temping Hole, then Burst Crack at level 28), or 1vs1 full STR with a knuckle (though the guide prefers AoE). If you're heading for Ringmaster, the Moon Beam style is already full INT, like the Hit-and-Run build (and INT stays the main stat in all three builds).

Recommended gear

Weapons

  • Stick — the Ringmaster's weapon: every one of its class skills requires one. In Flyff Universe every stick is two-handed, right up to level 190: no shield.
  • Knuckles — one-handed and limited to the Assist and its classes (a shield is possible), for the Assist's fist attacks: Temping Hole, Burst Crack, Power Fist. They can't cast Ringmaster skills.
  • Staff — Magician-only: a Ringmaster can't equip one.

Stick progression:

  • Level 60 — Guardian Stick (INT +7, max HP +15%)
  • Level 75 — Historic Stick (max MP +30%, casting time -15%)
  • Level 90 — Angel Stick (INT +15, max HP +20%)
  • Level 105 — Legendary Golden Stick for support (INT +15, casting time -20%, healing +10%) or Mythical Silver Stick for AoE (max HP +20%, STA +15, block +10%)
  • Level 120 — Bloody Stick (attack +10%, healing +10%, casting time -25%, HP steal +1%) or Bloody Obsidian Stick to tank (max HP +25%, STA +20, damage taken -8%)
  • Level 150 — Lusaka's Crystal Stick (attack and healing +12 to 17%, casting time -28 to 33%, INT +25 to 30) or Song of Sirens (attack and healing +10 to 15%, casting time -26 to 30%); Lusaka's Stick to tank
  • Level 175 (Seraph) — Cursed Oracle (attack and healing +12 to 17%, casting time -28 to 33%, INT +25 to 30)
  • Level 180 (Seraph) — Oracle (attack and healing +17 to 22%, casting time -33 to 42%, INT +30 to 39); Mirage for tanking and PvP

Beyond the model, the stick's awake matters too: on a unique stick it can give up to healing +25% for support, or up to +25% damage on Merkaba Hanzelrusha or Moon Beam for AoE and Hit-and-Run.

Jewelry

For support, Madrigal Inside recommends Intiret rings (INT +10 for the Intiret +1 from level 58, up to INT +50 for the Intiret +5 at level 118: separate items, not upgrade levels), Penzeru earrings (DEF and magic DEF +150 for the Penzeru +1 from level 60, up to +250 for the Penzeru +5 at level 100), an MP or HP necklace and the Cloak of Wisdom (INT +8; an item shop item that players can trade); for AoE, Cloak of Wisdom or Cloak of Endurance (STA +8, also from the item shop) depending on your INT/STA balance. Rotation and pacing

Once your buffs are up, a Ringmaster's rhythm comes down to five timings:

  • Before the long buffs expire — 250 s + 2 s per INT point for the Assist buffs at level 20 and for Protect and Spirit Fortune at level 10 (a little over 14 minutes at 300 INT): recast on each player the buffs their class actually uses. The full set costs 675 MP and 11 s of casting per player, and not every buff helps everyone: per the STR tooltip, Beef Up adds no attack to wands or bows. For AoE, Prevention, which you cast only on yourself, stays at 150 s + 2 s per INT point at every level (12 min 30 s at 300 INT): recast it before the others.
  • Every 30 s + 0.15 s per INT point — Geburah Tiphreth, the Ringmaster's only party buff (75 s at 300 INT, 105 s at 500 INT).
  • On your partner's target — Holycross (30 MP, no PvE cooldown): the next hit the target takes deals double damage; Madrigal Inside advises casting it as soon as your partner targets a monster.
  • As damage comes in — Heal Rain for the party (0.5 s cast, no cooldown in the game data), Heal for a player in danger.
  • In reaction — Gvur Tialla against a debuff, Holyguard or Barrier of Life on an ally about to take a beating, Resurrection if someone goes down.

For your buff set, Madrigal Inside advises casting Mental Sign on yourself first, so the INT you gain benefits the buffs that follow. Save Geburah Tiphreth for last, right before the fight: it lasts far less than the other buffs.

The first two timings are mechanical and lend themselves to automation, but not at the same pace (and single-target buffs land on whoever is selected when the cycle runs, so switching between players is still up to you); the other three need your attention. In the game, F2 shows your second shortcut bar, keys 1 to 0 fire its slots and F1 brings back the first bar. So put your long buffs on slots 1 to 0 of your second bar. That is 10 buffs per cycle at most, while the full buff set has 11, so keep the ones the target's class uses. Ringmaster vs Billposter — What's the difference?

At level 60, the Assist chooses between two classes:

  • Ringmaster = healing and buffs with a stick; its nine skills cost MP, and five of them also need Bless Posters; each INT point gives it twice as much max MP as a Billposter (16.2 vs 8.1). It can also level solo with an AoE or Hit-and-Run build. Third job: Seraph.
  • Billposter = melee combat with knuckles (Asmodeus strengthens the equipped weapon); its attacks such as Belial Smashing, Blood Fist or Sonichand cost FP. Third job: Forcemaster.

If you want to heal and strengthen others → Ringmaster. If you'd rather fight with your fists → Billposter.

Common Ringmaster beginner mistakes

  • Managing FP instead of MP — every Ringmaster skill costs MP (Heal Rain costs 80 to 170 MP, Geburah Tiphreth 155 to 250 depending on level): carry Refreshers (the Tenth Refresher restores 675 MP), not Vital Drinks (they restore FP). For long sessions, two item shop items help: Low Refresher Hold (MP use -50% for 1 h) and Refresher Hold (unlimited MP for 1 h).
  • Forgetting Bless Posters — Merkaba Hanzelrusha, Spirit Fortune, Geburah Tiphreth, Gvur Tialla and Barrier of Life use them up (50 penya each): without them those skills won't cast, even in an automated rotation.
  • Relying on a fixed buff duration — it depends on your INT and, for most buffs, on the skill level (Geburah Tiphreth stays at a 30 s base and Prevention at 150 s, whatever their level); recalculate your rebuff interval when your INT changes, and remember that Geburah Tiphreth runs out long before the others.
  • Mixing up buffs — Beef Up gives STR, not HP (that's Patience); Cat's Reflex gives block, not dodge.
  • Neglecting INT — heals, buffs, Merkaba damage and your MP pool all depend on it; only the AoE build raises its STA a little (50 to 120 depending on the guide).
  • Staying too far from the party — Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth target party members within 13 m: invite your allies to the party and stay close to them.
  • Looking for a shield or a staff — every stick is two-handed and staffs are Magician-only.
  • Thinking the Ringmaster can't kill — with an AoE or Hit-and-Run build, Merkaba Hanzelrusha (made about 28% stronger by patch 1.5.2 in April 2026) lets you level solo.
