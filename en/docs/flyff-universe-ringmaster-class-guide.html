What is a Ringmaster?

The Ringmaster (RM) is one of the Assist's two second-job classes in Flyff Universe, alongside the Billposter. It is the healing and buffing branch: its main job is keeping a group alive and making it stronger. That doesn't make it a mere healer, though: community guides also describe AoE and Hit-and-Run builds that let it level solo. Its nine Ringmaster skills are all cast with a stick and cost MP, never FP.

Class path

  • Levels 1 to 15: Vagrant, the class every character starts as.
  • Level 15: Assist (first job change, levels 15 to 60). This is where you learn the heals and buffs the Ringmaster keeps.
  • Level 60: Ringmaster (levels 60 to 165), or Billposter if you'd rather fight with your fists.
  • Level 165: Seraph, the third job added by the Muran's Awakening expansion (v1.5.0, November 27, 2025), up to the level cap, now 190. Its own skills unlock from level 166: Kyrie Eleison, Gloria Patri, Divinecross, Hammer of Judgement…

What a Ringmaster does

  • Heal: Heal (one ally, 650 + INT × 5.88 HP at level 20, 0.15 s cast), Circle Healing (party members within 15 m) and Heal Rain (party members within 13 m, from level 70). All of these heals grow with INT.
  • Buff: the Assist's nine stat buffs (STR, STA, DEX, INT, max HP, attack speed, movement speed, block, hit rate) plus the Ringmaster's own: Protect (defense), Spirit Fortune (damage), Holyguard (crit immunity) and Geburah Tiphreth, the party buff.
  • Revive: Resurrection, an Assist skill, brings a dead player back and reduces their EXP loss.
  • React: Gvur Tialla removes every debuff from an ally, Barrier of Life makes them block every hit for 1.2 to 2 s, and Holycross makes an enemy take double damage from the next hit it receives.

The Ringmaster's role in a group

Three main roles depending on context:

⚔️

Group leveling and farming

You back up one or more damage dealers. Your job: renew buffs before they expire, recast Geburah Tiphreth, which lasts far less than the others, heal with Heal Rain and Heal, and watch your MP. Stay within 13 m: that's the range of both Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth. The rebuffing part is highly repetitive — exactly where automation shines.

🐉

Dungeons & bosses

Instance dungeons open by difficulty tier: Leren Chasm (Flaris, from Easy), Rhisis Catacombs (Garden of Rhisis, from Medium), Envy Depths (Darkon 1 and 2, from Hard), Guardiane Sanctuary (Darkon 3, from Extra Hard), Storm Peak (Azria, from Nightmare), The Wilds (Coral Island, from Cursed) and Ankou's Asylum (Kaillun, on Infernal). Against bosses such as the Caveman Chief, the Crystal Demon Lord or Ankou, the rhythm changes: more targeted heals, defensive skills to time well (Holyguard, Barrier of Life, Gvur Tialla) and sometimes a Resurrection to plan for. Keep your hands on the heals.

🏆

Guild Siege and FWC

Guild Sieges are fought in level brackets: the level 60 bracket is for characters who haven't made their second job change (so a Ringmaster can't enter it), the level 80 bracket for players up to level 80, and the game's texts also include a level 165 bracket. The Flyff World Championship (FWC) has been held every year since 2023; the 2026 edition started on April 23, 2026.

Buffs: what they really raise

The Ringmaster keeps the Assist's buffs and adds its own. Every buff below targets a single player, except Geburah Tiphreth, which hits the whole party within 13 m. The Assist's nine stat buffs last 150 s (2:30) at level 1 and 250 s (4:10) at level 20; Protect and Spirit Fortune last 160 to 250 s. In every case, add 2 s per point of the caster's INT, with no cap. Example: at 300 INT, a max-level buff lasts 250 + 300 × 2 = 850 s, a little over 14 minutes. Geburah Tiphreth is the exception: 30 s + 0.15 s per INT point, whatever its level (75 s at 300 INT).

Support guides recommend maxing three of the Ringmaster's own buffs first:

Full Support, AoE or Hit-and-Run — Which build to choose?

Flyff Universe guides describe three builds:

BuildStat spreadWhat it's for
Full Support15 STR / 15 STA / 15 DEX, everything else in INTBuffing and healing a group or a leveling partner, with buffs and heals at their strongest.
AoE (facetank)Mostly INT, with about 50 STA (Madrigal Inside, 80 at most) to 100-120 STA (Flyff Universe Wiki)Leveling solo by gathering monsters around you, with Merkaba, Stonehand and Prevention.
Hit-and-Run (HnR)Full INTFlexible: the same stats work for support, dungeons and solo leveling, with no reset. Effective up to around level 120 according to Madrigal Inside.

What all three share: INT. Your heals, the strength and duration of your buffs, the damage of each Merkaba Hanzelrusha tick (INT × 4 to 4.18) and your MP pool all depend on it — each INT point gives a Ringmaster 16.2 max MP. The 500 INT figure comes up a lot: that's where every INT-based buff bonus hits its cap (Heap Up: 0.04 × 500 = +20 extra STA; Spirit Fortune: 0.4 × 500 = +200 extra damage; Geburah Tiphreth: attack +20% in total). Beyond that, INT keeps raising your heals, your MP and your buff durations.

Skill order

  • Full Support: Holycross first (a single point, required for Spirit Fortune), then max Protect, Spirit Fortune and Geburah Tiphreth. Geburah Tiphreth also requires Holyguard 4; beyond that, Holyguard matters mostly when you support an AoE player. Heal at level 3 is enough to support a single player, thanks to INT.
  • AoE: max Merkaba Hanzelrusha from level 60; keep Prevention (an automatic heal when your HP drops below 15% in PvE) and Stonehand (a stun chance on every attack, 12% in PvE at level 20) maxed; Protect early; Heal around level 3. Keep Geburah Tiphreth, Heap Up, Patience and Cat's Reflex up, and save Holyguard for big pulls. From level 105, guides point to the Darkon 3 desert with the Rimyth set.
  • Hit-and-Run (per Madrigal Inside): Merkaba Hanzelrusha for damage, Moon Beam maxed, Barrier of Life for safety and Geburah Tiphreth as the key buff.

Before level 60: the Assist

According to Madrigal Inside, an Assist levels one of two ways: Hit-and-Run with Moon Beam (full INT, stick; available from level 15, it deals damage over time and makes the target take up to 8% more damage), or STR AoE with a knuckle and a shield (Temping Hole, then Burst Crack at level 28). If you're heading for Ringmaster, the Moon Beam style is already full INT, like the Hit-and-Run build (and INT stays the main stat in all three builds).

Recommended gear

Weapons

  • Stick — the Ringmaster's weapon: every one of its class skills requires one. In Flyff Universe every stick is two-handed, right up to level 190: no shield.
  • Knuckles — one-handed and limited to the Assist and its classes (a shield is possible), for the Assist's fist attacks: Temping Hole, Burst Crack, Power Fist. They can't cast Ringmaster skills.
  • Staff — Magician-only: a Ringmaster can't equip one.

Stick progression:

  • Level 60 — Guardian Stick (INT +7, max HP +15%)
  • Level 75 — Historic Stick (max MP +30%, casting time -15%)
  • Level 90 — Angel Stick (INT +15, max HP +20%)
  • Level 105 — Legendary Golden Stick for support (INT +15, casting time -20%, healing +10%) or Mythical Silver Stick for AoE (max HP +20%, STA +15, block +10%)
  • Level 120 — Bloody Stick (attack +10%, healing +10%, casting time -25%, HP steal +1%) or Bloody Obsidian Stick to tank (max HP +25%, STA +20, damage taken -8%)

  • Level 180 — Oracle (attack and healing +17 to 22%, casting time -33 to 42%, INT +30 to 39); Mirage for tanking and PvP

Rotation and pacing

Once your buffs are up, a Ringmaster's rhythm comes down to four timings:

  • Before the long buffs expire — 250 s at max level + 2 s per INT point (a little over 14 minutes at 300 INT): recast the full set on each player.
  • Every 30 s + 0.15 s per INT point — Geburah Tiphreth, the only party buff (75 s at 300 INT, 105 s at 500 INT).
  • As damage comes in — Heal Rain for the party (0.5 s cast, no cooldown in the game data), Heal for a player in danger.
  • In reaction — Gvur Tialla against a debuff, Holyguard or Barrier of Life on an ally about to take a beating, Resurrection if someone goes down.

For your buff set, Madrigal Inside advises casting Mental Sign on yourself first, so the INT you gain benefits the buffs that follow. Save Geburah Tiphreth for last, right before the fight: it lasts far less than the other buffs.

Ringmaster vs Billposter — What's the difference?

At level 60, the Assist chooses between two classes:

  • Ringmaster = healing and buffs with a stick; its nine skills cost MP, and each INT point gives it twice as much max MP as a Billposter (16.2 vs 8.1). It can also level solo with an AoE or Hit-and-Run build. Third job: Seraph.
  • Billposter = melee combat with knuckles (Asmodeus strengthens the equipped weapon); its attacks such as Belial Smashing, Blood Fist or Sonichand cost FP. Third job: Forcemaster.

The Mentalist, sometimes named in its place, is actually the Psykeeper's third job on the Magician line: it has nothing to do with the Assist. If you want to heal and strengthen others → Ringmaster. If you'd rather fight with your fists → Billposter.

Common Ringmaster beginner mistakes

  • Managing FP instead of MP — every Ringmaster skill costs MP (Heal Rain costs 80 to 170 MP, Geburah Tiphreth 155 to 250 depending on level): carry MP potions (Refreshers), not Vital Drinks.
  • Relying on a fixed buff duration — it depends on the skill level and your INT; recalculate your rebuff interval when your INT changes, and remember that Geburah Tiphreth runs out long before the others.
  • Mixing up buffs — Beef Up gives STR, not HP (that's Patience); Cat's Reflex gives block, not dodge.
  • Neglecting INT — heals, buffs, Merkaba damage and your MP pool all depend on it; only the AoE build raises its STA a little (50 to 120 depending on the guide).
  • Staying too far from the party — Heal Rain and Geburah Tiphreth target party members within 13 m: invite your allies to the party and stay close to them.
  • Looking for a shield or a staff — every stick is two-handed and staffs are Magician-only.
  • Thinking the Ringmaster can't kill — with an AoE or Hit-and-Run build, Merkaba Hanzelrusha (made about 28% stronger by patch 1.5.2 in April 2026) lets you level solo.
