Flyff Universe Keyboard Shortcuts — Complete 2026 List

Every key in the game gathered in a single reference. Combat, buffs, inventory, chat, navigation: the setup every player should know by heart before tackling end-game.

Why mastering shortcuts changes everything

On Flyff Universe, a player who uses the mouse for every action loses on average 30 to 40% of their DPS compared to a player who masters their keyboard shortcuts. The reason is mathematical: a click takes 250-400 ms (movement + precision), a keystroke takes 80-120 ms. Across a 5-minute boss fight requiring 200 actions, this difference is the gap between a wipe and a kill.

This guide collects default shortcuts, configurable shortcuts, and the recommended setup per class. Everything you need to never open a menu with your mouse mid-fight again.

💡 AZERTY vs QWERTY Flyff Universe respects your physical keyboard layout. On a French AZERTY, A and Q are swapped compared to QWERTY. If you use an assistant like Reborn Assistant, set the layout in Settings — otherwise your macros will trigger the wrong keys.

Hidden & useful shortcuts

Lesser-known shortcuts that can save you while farming:

  • Ctrl+left click on an inventory item: quick-move to opened bank or trade window
  • Shift+left click on a stackable item: opens the quantity dialog (handy to sell 50 out of 200)
  • Shift+left click on a player name in chat: opens a direct whisper
  • Hold Alt: shows player names overlay (useful in crowded zones)
  • Double-click on an active buff icon (top of screen): cancels that buff immediately

Recommended setup per class

Ringmaster / Mentalist (support)

You spend 80% of your time on buffs. The F2 bar becomes your headquarters:

  • F1 — combat bar (target/Heal Rain/HP potion)
  • F2 — slots 1-9: Heart of Power, Beef Up, Mental Sign, Speed Pep, Cat's Reflex, Holy Cross, other buffs
  • F3 — Resurrection, dispels, situationals
  • F4 — stacked FP/HP potions
  • R or T — dedicated Heal Rain (free key for Reborn Assistant automation)

Blade / Knight (melee DPS)

Fast combos, fluid target switching:

  • F1 — Sonic Blade, Cross Strike, Berserk (the hits that build DPS)
  • F2 — Auto-attack toggle, parry, blink-away
  • Tab — auto-target nearest mob (reflex on pull)
  • 1-4 — HP potions in quick-access

Mage / Psykeeper (caster)

Long cast time, mandatory kite:

  • F1 — main cast spells (Fire Strike, Lightning Mastery, etc.)
  • F2 — AOE spells
  • F3 — Spell Mastery / activatable passives
  • Space — kite jump (casters live by their mobility)

Ranger / Jester (ranged DPS)

  • F1 — Stunning Throw, Pulling Arrow, main hits
  • F2 — Critical Swing, personal buffs
  • F3 — Positioning skills (escape, dash)
  • Tab — target reset after each pull

FAQ Flyff Universe shortcuts

Can I remap F1, F2, F3, F4?

No, these keys are fixed and tied to the four action bars. The entire community macro and automation ecosystem relies on this convention.

My shortcuts don't work in fullscreen browser?

Check that the game window has focus (click on it once). On some browsers, the iframe loses focus when another Chrome window is active. Solution: open the game in a separate window (not a tab), via right-click → "Open in new window".

AZERTY or QWERTY for Flyff Universe?

Neither is better, it's just your physical keyboard. On AZERTY, the Z/Q/S/D keys replace W/A/S/D. Reborn Assistant detects your layout automatically if you set it in Settings.

How do I show the shortcut list in-game?

Type /keys or /key in chat, or open System → Keys. The full list appears, with the option to customize the non-server-locked keys.
