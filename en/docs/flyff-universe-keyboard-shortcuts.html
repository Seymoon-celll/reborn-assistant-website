Flyff Universe Keyboard Shortcuts — 2026 Guide

Flyff Universe's default keys, checked one by one: shortcut bars, windows, camera, chat — and how to rebind them to suit your hands.

Why learn the shortcuts

In Flyff Universe, the keyboard covers the essentials: keys 1 to 0 use the skills and items stored in your shortcut bar, F1 to F9 switch from one bar to another, and the main windows (inventory, skills, party…) each have their own key. Knowing them saves you from hunting for a menu with the mouse mid-fight and keeps your hands on your target and your movement.

This guide collects the default keys, verified against the game's official texts (tutorial, help, keybinding window), how to rebind them and example layouts per class. Anything that could not be verified was left out.

💡 AZERTY keyboards The keys listed here are the ones the game's texts give, for example W A S D to move, which is the layout of a QWERTY keyboard. No official source says how the game adapts them to a French AZERTY keyboard: open Options › Controls, “Keys” window, and check the “Move Forward”, “Move Backward”, “Turn Left” and “Turn Right” actions. If you use Reborn Assistant, set exactly the same keys there as in the game.

Chat & communication

Prefixes go at the start of your message:

KeyAction
EnterOpen chat / send message
/sShout: wider reach than normal chat. Other players only see your shouts once you have reached level 20.
/pTalk to your party
/gTalk to your guild
/w NameWhisper: private message to the character “Name”
/partyinvite NameInvite the character “Name” to your party

A regular shout does not cover the whole channel. To be heard across the whole channel, or on every channel, you need an item: Full Shout, Channel Shout or Server Shout.

Mouse & lesser-known shortcuts

A few official commands players often discover late:

  • Shift+left click (or right click) on a container item: shows its contents
  • Left click on a nearby player: opens their menu (trade, add to messenger, party or guild invite, follow, duel…). The “Right-Click Player Menu” option in Options › Game moves it to the right click.
  • Double-click a monster: attack it. In Options › Game, “Single Click to Attack” replaces the double-click with a single click, and “Auto Attack” keeps your basic attacks going on their own.
  • Cancel one of your buffs: possible, but the game asks for confirmation, since it cannot be undone
  • Scroll Lock on the world map: changes the map's zoom level
  • Fullscreen, hidden UI, screenshot mode: these actions each have a line in the “Keys” window (“Toggle Fullscreen”, “Hide/Show UI”, “Screenshot Mode”); check their key there or set your own

Example layouts per class

The game gives no role to F1, F2 or F3: the layouts below are suggestions to adapt to your build. They only name skills that actually exist in Flyff Universe.

Ringmaster (support)

The heal and buff branch of the Assist (3rd class: Seraph). Its skills, like the Assist buffs, cost MP and never FP: stock MP potions rather than FP ones.

  • F1 — combat and healing: Heal Rain (party heal), Geburah Tiphreth (short party buff, recast often), Merkaba Hanzelrusha if you play AoE, HP and MP potions
  • F2 — single-target buffs: Protect, Spirit Fortune, Heap Up, Mental Sign, Beef Up, Haste, Patience, Cat's Reflex…
  • F3 — situational: Resurrection (revives a dead player), Gvur Tialla (removes all debuffs from the target), Holyguard (protects the target from critical hits for a few seconds), Holycross (debuff: the next hit the enemy takes deals double damage)
  • Heal Rain on a dedicated slot — if you prefer a letter, rebind that slot's key in Options › Controls, avoiding T, which opens the Status window. Handy if you automate it with Reborn Assistant.

Blade (melee, two weapons)

Sonic Blade, Cross Strike and Berserk are Blade skills: a Knight cannot use them.

  • F1 — Sonic Blade (area attack, lv. 70) and Cross Strike (lv. 75), your main hits: you need two weapons equipped to use them
  • F2 — Berserk (lv. 65), a self-buff rather than an attack: more attack speed and striking power, less skill damage
  • 1 to 4 — HP potions, within easy reach on your combat bar
  • Auto attack — not a bar skill: it is a setting in Options › Game (“Auto Attack”)

Magician & Elementor (spells)

For spellcasters of the Magician line:

  • F1 — attack spells, for example Fire Strike (Magician spell, lv. 40, wand or staff)
  • F2 — your area spells
  • No slot for masteries — Lightning Mastery and the other Elementor masteries are passives: they cannot be cast or placed on a bar
  • Space — jump

Jester

  • F1 — your main attacks
  • F2 — Critical Swing (lv. 65, self-buff: critical chance and block penetration), your other self-buffs
  • F3 — Escape (lv. 60), on an easy-to-reach key

FAQ Flyff Universe shortcuts

Can I rebind F1–F9 and keys 1–0?

Yes. In Options › Controls, the “Keys” window lists bar switching (“Taskbar …”) and every slot (“Taskbar Slot …”). You can give each of these actions a different key; “Unbind key” leaves it without one and “Reset” restores the default keys.

My shortcuts don't respond in the browser?

First make sure the game window has focus: click inside it once. If you changed keys, check them in Options › Controls, “Keys” window; the “Reset” button restores the defaults. Fullscreen has its own line in that same window.

AZERTY or QWERTY for Flyff Universe?

The game's official texts don't say how it handles an AZERTY keyboard. The safest approach: open the “Keys” window, check the movement actions (“Move Forward”, “Move Backward”, “Turn Left”, “Turn Right”) and give them the keys that suit you. Reborn Assistant presses the keys you tell it to: set the same ones there as in the game.

How do I see the shortcut list in-game?

Open the Options, “Controls” tab: the “Keys” window lists the actions by group (movement, Action Slot, targeting, windows, camera, taskbars, fullscreen…) and lets you rebind them.

Is there a key to target the nearest monster?

No official game text describes one. You select a monster by clicking it and attack it with a double-click, or a single click with the “Single Click to Attack” option. The only targeting action in the “Keys” window is “Cycle Targets (PvP)”, meant for PvP.
